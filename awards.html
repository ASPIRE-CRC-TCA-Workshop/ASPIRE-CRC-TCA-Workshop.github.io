Building a Connected Future: Sustainable and Intelligent Communications

ASPIRE-CRC-TCA International Workshop

August 10–11, 2026

IIC 2001, Bruneau Centre for Research and Innovation, Memorial University, St. John's, NL, Canada

Awards

There will be two best presentation awards for the Ph.D. track and two best presentation awards for the Master's track. The recipients will be announced during the workshop's closing ceremony.

Evaluation Rubric

Each presentation is scored on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) across six criteria.

CriterionDescriptionMax Score
Technical QualityDepth, accuracy, and relevance of the technical content5
OrganizationLogical flow, structure, and coherence of the presentation5
ClarityClear explanation of concepts and effective communication5
Visual AidsQuality and effectiveness of slides and visual materials5
Q&AAbility to answer questions and engage with the audience5
Time ManagementEffective use of allotted presentation time5
Total30

Scoring Scale: 1 = Poor — 2 = Fair — 3 = Good — 4 = Very Good — 5 = Excellent
